## Description
CTeen Great Neck is the teen chapter at Chabad of Great Neck, led by Rabbi Zevy and Shani Geisinsky. This is the chapter's own app — made for the teens who come to events, keep up with the feed, and take on the challenges.

What's inside:

• Events — See what's coming up in Great Neck and around CTeen. RSVP in one tap, add an event to your calendar, and get directions. We'll remind you before it starts.

• Calendar — A live month view of everything the chapter has on, so you can see the whole picture at a glance.

• Feed — Photos and announcements from Rabbi Zevy and the leaders, with likes and comments.

• The Tefillin Challenge — Keep your streak, snap your daily photo, and climb toward the prize.

• Your account — Your RSVPs, saved events and streak in one place. Sign in with Apple to keep everything if you switch phones.

• Notifications — A heads-up when sign-ups open, when plans change, and when it's time to keep your streak. You're always in control of them in Settings.

Made for the teens of CTeen Great Neck.
